Food Chain Defense Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-18

Overview

Food Chain Defense is a local-action defense game. The app stores gameplay progress, settings, and rewarded-ad cooldown state on the device so the game can continue working between sessions.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads and optional rewarded video ads using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, diagnostics, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads.

Learn more from Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data We Store

  • Local game progress and session state
  • Rewarded ad cooldown timing
  • App settings needed for gameplay

Accounts

This app does not require account sign-in and does not sync saves to a developer-operated backend.

Contact

For privacy or account-related requests, use the developer contact information listed on this app's Google Play page.
